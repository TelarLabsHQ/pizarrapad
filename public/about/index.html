About PizarraPad

PizarraPad is a free online board for teachers, made by Telar Labs, a small independent studio.

Many classrooms have a projector or TV but no expensive interactive board. Teachers end up using slides that can't be written on, or apps with sign-ups, page limits and watermarks. PizarraPad gives every teacher a board they can write on with whatever they have: a laptop and mouse, a tablet and stylus, or a phone.

The name comes from pizarra, the Spanish word for blackboard.

Our promises

  • The board stays free, with no sign-up.
  • Your boards stay on your device.
  • No ads on the board while you teach.

Other Telar Labs tools
