Frequently asked questions

Is PizarraPad really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no sign-up and no page limit. The information pages show a few ads to cover costs; the board itself never shows ads.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the board and start teaching.

Where is my work saved?

In your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Clearing your browser data will remove saved boards, so save important pages as images.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After you open the board once while online, it keeps working offline in the same browser.

Which styluses work?

Any stylus your device supports as a pen: Samsung S Pen, Apple Pencil, Surface Pen, Wacom and others. Pressure sensitivity works where the device provides it.

Can students use it?

Yes, it works on any device with a browser. Each device keeps its own boards.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. In Chrome or Edge choose "Install app" or "Add to home screen"; on iPad/iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Can I add pictures?

Yes. Use the Image tool to add a photo, diagram or worksheet picture, then write on top of it. Pictures are saved on your device with the board.

Which languages does the board support?

English, Spanish (Español) and Simplified Chinese (中文). Change it under ⋯ → Language.

How do I undo a mistake?

Tap Undo, or press Ctrl+Z on a keyboard. Redo brings it back. Even Clear page can be undone.

What is Pen only mode?

With Pen only turned on, only a stylus draws. Your palm and fingers scroll and zoom instead, so you can rest your hand on the screen while you write, like on paper.

Which backgrounds are there?

Three boards (whiteboard, blackboard and greenboard), each with a plain, lined or grid background. Lines help with handwriting and grids with math, graphs and tables.

How many pages can I make?

As many as your lesson needs. Use the arrows to move between pages. Pages are saved on your device until you start a new board.

How do I share a page with students?

Use Save page as image, then send the picture in your class chat, email or learning system. Students who were absent can see exactly what was on the board.

How do I start fresh for the next class?

Choose Start a new board. It erases all pages on this device, so save any page you need as an image first. To clear just one page, use Clear page.

Can I show it on a projector or TV?

Yes. Connect your laptop with a cable, or mirror a tablet or phone to a TV. See Use with a projector for step-by-step setups and fixes for common problems.

Does it work on cheap tablets and older laptops?

Usually, yes. It runs in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Samsung Internet or Firefox) and doesn’t need a fast connection after the first visit.

Can I use my phone with the projector computer?

Not yet. Writing on your phone or tablet while the board shows on the projector computer is coming soon, along with a library of maps and worksheets.
