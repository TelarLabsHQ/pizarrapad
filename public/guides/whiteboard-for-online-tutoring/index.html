How to Use an Online Whiteboard for Tutoring Over Zoom, Google Meet or Teams

In online tutoring and classes, a board you can write on makes explanations much clearer than talking over slides. You can share PizarraPad in any video call app, because it’s just a browser tab.

Step 1: Open the board

Open PizarraPad in your browser before the call. Choose a board and background under ⋯. Light chalk colors on the blackboard or greenboard are easy to read on small phone screens; try a white board too, and keep whichever looks clearer in your call.

Step 2: Share it in the call

Share only the board, not your whole screen, so students don’t see your other tabs and notifications.

AppHow to share the board
ZoomPress Share screen and choose the browser window with PizarraPad.
Google MeetPress Present now (or the share screen button) and choose A tab, then the PizarraPad tab.
Microsoft TeamsPress Share and choose the window with PizarraPad.

Video call apps change their buttons from time to time, but every one of them can share a window or a tab.

Step 3: Choose how you’ll write

  • Mouse or trackpad: fine for arrows, boxes and short labels. Use the Text tool to type longer notes; typed text is easier to read than mouse handwriting.
  • Tablet and stylus: the best way to write naturally. Join the call on the tablet too, and share the tablet’s screen with PizarraPad open. Turn on Pen only so your palm doesn’t leave marks.
  • Drawing tablet: a small, inexpensive pen tablet plugged into your laptop works like a pen for the mouse pointer.

If you join the same call on two devices, mute the microphone and speaker on one of them to avoid echo.

Tips for a clear online lesson

  • Write bigger than you think. Students may be on a small phone screen. Use the Thick pen and Large text.
  • Use the laser pointer to show where you are while you talk. Students can’t see your finger on the screen.
  • One idea per page. Add pages with + instead of erasing. You can go back to an earlier page when a student asks.
  • Pause after writing. Video can be a second or two behind. Give students time to see the step before you move on.
  • Add a picture of the student’s homework or a textbook page with the Image tool, and mark it up together.

Step 4: Share the notes afterwards

Save each page with ⋯ → Save page as image and send the pictures in your chat or email after the session. Students then have the exact notes from the lesson to review, and parents can see what was covered.

Privacy

Your boards are saved only in your browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers, and there is no account. Only what you share in the call is seen by students.
