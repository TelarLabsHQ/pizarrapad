Everything you need at the front of the class

Three boards

Switch between a clean whiteboard, a classic charcoal blackboard and a green chalkboard, with lined or grid backgrounds for handwriting and math.

Any pen, finger or mouse

Works with a Samsung S Pen, Apple Pencil, Surface Pen and other styluses (with pressure), your finger, a mouse, or the keyboard.

Pen-only mode

Turn it on and your palm and fingers stop drawing. Fingers scroll and zoom instead, so you can rest your hand on the screen.

Type when you prefer

Tap with the Text tool and type. Change the size, color or bold, and drag text to move it.

Shapes, highlighter, laser

Straight lines, arrows, boxes and circles; a see-through highlighter; and a laser pointer that fades away.

Add pictures

Insert a photo, diagram or worksheet picture, or snap one with your phone camera. Move and resize it, then write on top.

Pages and saving

Add as many pages as your lesson needs. Everything saves on your device automatically, and you can save any page as an image.

Works offline

Once opened, the board keeps working even when the school Wi-Fi drops.

English, Español, 中文

The board speaks English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese, and picks your device language automatically.

Free, no account

No sign-up, no limits on pages, no watermark. Open it and teach.

Start in three steps

  1. Open the board

    Go to the board on your laptop, tablet or phone. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

  2. Pick your board and tools

    Tap ⋯ to choose whiteboard, blackboard or greenboard and plain, lined or grid. Pick the pen, color and thickness in the tool bar.

  3. Show it to the class

    Connect your laptop to the projector or TV, or mirror your tablet. See the options.
