Privacy policy

PizarraPad is run by Telar Labs. In short: your boards stay on your device, and we don't ask who you are.

Your boards

Everything you write is saved in your own browser's storage on your device. It is not sent to us. If you clear your browser data, your saved boards are deleted.

No accounts

We don't ask for your name, email or school to use the board.

Advertising

Some information pages show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first. The board page itself shows no ads.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, whose servers may record basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the service secure and working.

Children

Teachers and students of all ages can use the board. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the board page doesn't show ads.
