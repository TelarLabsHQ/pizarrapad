Animal race

How to play the animal race

  1. Type the racers, one per line: student names, teams or even answer choices. Up to 12 can race.
  2. Each racer gets a cute animal: a duck, bunny, turtle, cat, piggy, frog, chick, penguin, bear, fox, mouse or panda. Shuffle the animals to swap them.
  3. Press Start the race. After “3, 2, 1, GO!”, the animals hop to the finish line, overtaking each other along the way.
  4. The winner gets 🥇, then 🥈 and 🥉. Press Race again for another round.

Is the race fair?

Yes. The finishing order is drawn with your browser’s secure random numbers when you press Start, so every racer has the same chance. The overtaking you see is just for fun, and the slow turtle wins as often as the fox.

Ideas for class

  • Who goes first: presentations, games or lining up.
  • Team points: race the teams; 1st place earns 3 points, 2nd 2 points, 3rd 1 point.
  • Predict the winner: before the race, students write down who they think will win. A fun way to talk about chance and probability.
  • Answer race: type four answer choices as the racers for a light-hearted “which one wins?” before revealing the right answer.
  • Party games and raffles at class parties.
