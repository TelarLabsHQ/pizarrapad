Classroom dice

Dice:

Tip: press the space bar to roll.

How to use the dice

  1. Choose how many dice to roll, from 1 to 6.
  2. Press Roll or the space bar. With two or more dice, the total appears underneath.
  3. Press Full screen to show the dice big on the projector, so everyone sees the same roll.

Math games with dice

  • Addition race: roll two dice and add. Older students can roll three and add, or multiply two.
  • Make the biggest number: roll three dice and arrange the digits to make the largest (or smallest) three-digit number.
  • Race to 100: teams take turns rolling and adding to their total. First to reach 100 exactly wins.
  • Probability: roll two dice 30 times as a class and tally the totals. Why does 7 come up more often than 2 or 12?
  • Fractions: roll two dice and make a fraction with the smaller number on top. Compare fractions with a partner.

Dice for every subject

  • Language: number six question words (who, what, where, when, why, how) and roll to choose which one students answer about a story.
  • Review games: number six topics and roll to choose the next review question.
  • Writing prompts: roll for a character, a place and a problem from three lists, then write a short story.
  • Brain breaks: number six exercises (jumping jacks, stretches, balance) and roll to pick the next one.

Why digital dice in class?

Physical dice roll off desks, get lost and are hard for a whole class to see. Big on-screen dice are fair, visible to everyone and always ready. Pair them with the PizarraPad board to write down the results or keep score.

Questions

Are the rolls really random? Yes. Each die uses the browser’s random number generator, so every face is equally likely.

Is it free? Yes, with no sign-up, and it works offline once the page has loaded.
