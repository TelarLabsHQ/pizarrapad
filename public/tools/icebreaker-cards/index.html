How to use the icebreaker cards

  1. Choose a deck: Would you rather, This or that, Finish the sentence, Get to know you, Quick draw, Two truths and a lie, or My own cards.
  2. Press Next card (or the space bar) to show a big card on the projector. Cards come in a random order, with no repeats until the deck runs out.
  3. Use the timer for thinking or drawing time. It starts by itself in Quick draw and Two truths and a lie. Choose 30, 60, 90 or 120 seconds next to the timer button.

Ways to play

  • Would you rather / This or that: students move to one side of the room for each answer, or show a thumbs up or down. Ask one or two to explain why.
  • Finish the sentence: go around the circle, or pair up and share for one minute each.
  • Get to know you: partners interview each other, then introduce their partner to the class.
  • Quick draw: one student draws the prompt on the PizarraPad board while the class guesses. No words or letters allowed!
  • Two truths and a lie: a student shares three statements, and the class votes on the lie.
  • My own cards: type review questions, vocabulary or discussion topics for any subject.

When to use icebreakers

  • The first days of school, and with a new class or group.
  • Brain breaks between long tasks, or after a Pomodoro round.
  • Morning meetings, advisory and homeroom.
  • Team building in clubs, youth groups and workshops for adults.

All the cards are written to be friendly and comfortable for every age. Students can always say “pass”.
