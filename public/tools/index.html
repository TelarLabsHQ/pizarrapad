Classroom tools

Free classroom tools for your projector or TV. They open instantly, work offline once loaded, and need no sign-up.

Classroom timerA big countdown for the projector, with color warnings and a chime.Random name pickerPick students fairly, without repeats, and make random groups.Classroom diceRoll 1 to 6 big dice for math games and review, with totals.Noise meterA traffic light that shows when the room is getting too loud.WhiteboardWrite, draw and teach on a whiteboard, blackboard or greenboard.

Made for the front of the classroom

Every tool has a Full screen button and big, high-contrast text that’s easy to read from the back of the room. They work on a laptop connected to a projector, a smart TV browser, a tablet or a phone.

Private by design

Your class list, your last timer setting and your dice choice are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to us. The noise meter measures loudness only and never records sound.

Use them with the board

Open the PizarraPad board in one tab and a tool in another, and switch between them during the lesson. For more ideas, see our guides for teachers.
