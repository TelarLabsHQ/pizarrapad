Random name picker

Ready?

How to use the random name picker

  1. Type or paste your class list into the box, one name per line. You can copy a column straight from a spreadsheet.
  2. Press Pick a name. The names spin for a moment and stop on one.
  3. Leave Don’t pick the same name twice on, and everyone gets a turn before anyone is picked again.
  4. Press Full screen to show it big on the projector. The list hides so only the name shows.

Your list is saved on this device, so it’s there next time. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Make random groups

Choose a group size and press Make groups. The picker shuffles your class and splits it into groups as evenly as possible. If the class doesn’t divide exactly, some groups get one extra person. Press it again for a new mix.

Why teachers pick names at random

  • Everyone stays ready. When anyone might be asked, the whole class keeps thinking, not just the students who raise their hands.
  • It feels fair. Students accept a random pick more easily than a choice that might look like favoritism.
  • Quieter students get a voice. A random picker gently includes students who rarely volunteer.
  • It saves time. No more deciding who goes first, who presents, or who hands out the books.

Tips for kind cold-calling

  • Give think time first. Ask the question, wait 10 to 20 seconds (try the timer), then pick a name.
  • Let students “phone a friend”. If the picked student is stuck, they can ask a classmate and then answer in their own words.
  • Use pair talk. Let partners discuss first, then pick one person to share their pair’s idea.
  • Keep the stakes low. Use it for sharing ideas and opinions more than for tricky right-or-wrong answers.

Other uses

  • Choosing who presents first, who leads a group, or the order of turns.
  • Picking a book, a topic, a warm-up activity or a game: just type those instead of names.
  • Prize draws and raffles at school events.

Questions

Is my class list private? Yes. The names stay in this browser on this device. We never see them.

Can I use it on a phone? Yes. It works on phones, tablets and computers.
