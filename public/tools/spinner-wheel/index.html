Spinner wheel

How to use the spinner wheel

  1. Type the choices under the wheel, one per line: names, prizes, topics, questions or groups. Up to about 100 fit.
  2. Press Spin, tap the middle of the wheel, or press the space bar. The wheel slows down with a ticking sound and lands on a random choice.
  3. Tick Remove the winner after each spin so nobody is picked twice. Put removed items back starts over.
  4. Use Full screen on the projector, and pick Bright, Pastel or Chalk colors.

Ideas for the wheel

  • Who answers next: your class list, with “remove the winner” on, so everyone gets a turn.
  • Review games: spin for the topic (“Fractions”, “Decimals”, “Word problems”) or for points (“1 point”, “2 points”, “Double!”, “Lose a turn”).
  • Prizes and raffles at a class party or a school event.
  • Writing prompts and debate topics.
  • Brain breaks: “10 jumping jacks”, “Dance for 30 seconds”, “Stretch”, “Tell a joke”.
  • Jobs and roles: who leads the group, who writes, who presents.

Is it fair?

Yes. The winner is chosen with your browser’s secure random numbers before the wheel starts turning, so every choice has the same chance, and the spin itself is just for show. To give an item more chances, type it on more than one line.

Questions

Is my list saved?

Yes, in this browser only, so it’s ready next time. Nothing is sent to us.

Want a race instead?

Try the animal race: each student gets a cute animal, and the winner crosses the finish line first.
