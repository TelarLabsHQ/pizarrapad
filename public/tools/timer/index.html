Classroom timer

5:00

Choose a time, then press Start. Tip: press the space bar to start or pause.

How to use the classroom timer

  1. Choose a time: 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes. Use + 1 min and − 1 min for anything in between.
  2. Press Full screen so the whole class can see it on the projector or TV.
  3. Press Start, or the space bar. The bar turns orange in the last minute and red in the last 10 seconds.
  4. When time is up, a short chime plays and the numbers flash. Turn the sound off if you prefer a silent timer.

You can add a minute while the timer is running, which is handy when a group needs “just a little longer”.

Ways teachers use a visible timer

  • Think time: “You have 2 minutes to think on your own before we share.” A timer keeps think time honest and calm.
  • Group work: 10 or 15 minutes per task, so groups pace themselves and you don’t have to keep announcing the time.
  • Transitions: 3 minutes to pack up, move desks or clean up. Students know exactly how long they have.
  • Quizzes and quick writes: a 5-minute warm-up quiz or journal entry at the start of class.
  • Stations: rotate every 8 or 10 minutes. The chime tells everyone to move.
  • Breaks: a 5-minute stretch or brain break that ends on time.

Tips for timers that work

  • Say the time out loud and show it. Some students rely on hearing it, others on seeing it.
  • Give a 1-minute warning. The color change helps, but a quick “one minute left” prepares everyone.
  • Use slightly less time than you think. Tasks expand to fill the time. You can always add a minute.
  • Keep it visible. On a projector, full screen works best. On a shared screen, put the timer next to your board.

Use it with the board

Write the task on the PizarraPad board, then open the timer in a second tab and switch between them. On a computer, you can place the two windows side by side.

Questions

Does it work offline? Yes. Once the page has loaded, the timer keeps running even if the Wi-Fi drops.

Does it keep running if I switch tabs? Yes. The timer uses the clock, so it stays accurate even if the page is in the background.

Is it free? Yes, with no sign-up. Your last chosen time is remembered on this device.
